Feasibility, cost & who pays

A trust layer the sector can afford, and that improves as it's used.

Who pays

  • Free single-site forecasts (adoption)
  • Bankable Demand Report per site for developers and lenders (proposed ~$300, under 0.1% of a ~$585k mini-grid)
  • Portfolio screening subscriptions for DFIs and RBF programmes
  • Households pay nothing; each report checks their bill against the AMDA ARPU benchmark

Running cost: open data, no API keys, runs on a laptop or a free cloud tier.

Demand Bureau: safe meter-data sharing

  • Give-to-get, borrowed from credit bureaus: share site-month aggregates, get benchmarks and narrower bands
  • No customer data; release only cells with ≥3 sites from ≥2 operators
  • Federated option: share fitted parameters, not series
  • Neutral steward (e.g. AMDA); a pooling clause in RBF grants seeds it

Forecast ledger

Borrowed from weather-forecast verification and clinical-trial registries: every forecast is hashed and timestamped before the site is built, then scored publicly when meters report. Trust earned in the open, not claimed.

Scale & impact

Built for Africa first. Works anywhere there are rooftops.

How it travels

  • Building footprints and OpenStreetMap cover all of Africa, plus South/Southeast Asia and Latin America
  • Demoed in Nigeria and Kenya, validated in DRC; a new country needs only its regional benchmark, then Bureau data calibrates it
  • Open source and self-hostable: data sovereignty for governments, no vendor lock-in
  • Flags settlements too small for a mini-grid (solar home systems instead) and sites near the grid

Access · Affordability · Availability

  • Access: a defensible P90 is what gets the next site financed
  • Affordability: right-sizing avoids stranded capital that ends up in the tariff. Every 10% of avoidable oversizing across the 217k mini-grids needed is ~$12.7B of capital (on ESMAP's $127B; illustrative)
  • Availability: sizing against the range, not a guess, prevents the evening brownouts of undersized systems

Lodestar makes demand bankable, so the next 200,000 mini-grids get built at the right size.
